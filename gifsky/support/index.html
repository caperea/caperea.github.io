How can we help?

Gifsky is a focused, on-device video-to-GIF converter for iPhone and iPad.

Choose a video

Tap Choose Video and select a locally available video or screen recording from Apple’s system Photos picker.

Conversion takes time

Longer clips, higher resolution, higher frame rate, and higher quality require more processing. You can cancel and try a shorter range or lower setting.

Save to Photos

Gifsky requests add-only Photos permission only when you choose Save to Photos. You can also use Share or Copy without granting full-library access.

Manage GIFs

Open a GIF in Gallery to zoom, view its file size, share, copy, save, or delete it. Long-press a gallery item to select multiple GIFs.

Privacy

Read the Gifsky Privacy Policy. Gifsky has no accounts, ads, tracking, or third-party analytics, and video-to-GIF conversion happens on device.
