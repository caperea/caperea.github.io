字课支持

字课帮助家庭把纸笔听写、逐字记录和薄弱字复习连成一个清楚的过程。

需要帮助？ 请发送邮件至 badtoyx@gmail.com。建议附上设备型号、iOS 版本、字课版本，以及问题出现前的操作步骤；请勿发送孩子姓名或其他不必要的个人信息。

快速上手

  1. 按年级、学期、单元、字表或熟练度选择听写范围。
  2. 家长报词，孩子在纸上书写。
  3. 为每个字标记“轻松”“费劲”或“写错”。
  4. 在错字本和统计中查看薄弱字，并用于下一轮复习。

数据与备份

核心学习数据保存在设备本地。更换设备、重装 App 或进行重要升级前，建议先在字课设置中导出备份，并确认文件已保存到你选择的位置。恢复时，请在设置中选择对应的备份文件。

语言说明

字课 App 内界面、字词内容和教材结构始终使用简体中文，不随系统语言切换。海外商店页面的本地化说明不代表 App 提供对应语言的界面。

兼容性

ZiKe Support

ZiKe connects paper dictation, character-by-character results, and focused review of weaker characters.

Need help? Email badtoyx@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, ZiKe version, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not include a child's name or other personal information that is not needed to resolve the problem.

Getting started

  1. Choose a range by grade, semester, unit, character list, or mastery.
  2. A parent reads the prompt while the learner writes on paper.
  3. Mark every character as Easy, Hard, or Wrong.
  4. Use the Mistake Notebook and statistics to bring weaker characters into the next review.

Data and backups

Core learning data is stored locally on your device. Before changing devices, reinstalling the app, or making an important upgrade, export a backup from ZiKe settings and confirm that the file was saved where you intended. To restore, choose the relevant backup file in settings.

Language

ZiKe's in-app interface, character and word content, and curriculum structure remain in Simplified Chinese in every system language. Localized App Store information does not mean that the app offers an interface in that language.

Compatibility

The current version is designed for iPhone. System requirements and availability are determined by the compatibility information shown by the App Store on your device.
