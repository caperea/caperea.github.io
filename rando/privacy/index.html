Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Rando stores your configurations, preferences, and recent results on your device. We do not operate user accounts or data servers, and Rando does not use ads, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

1. Data Rando handles

To provide reusable random pickers and result history, Rando stores data you create locally on your device. This may include number ranges, word lists, symbol selections, configuration names, recent results, and app preferences.

Rando does not upload this data to developer servers, sell it, or use it for advertising profiles.

2. Network access, analytics, and tracking

  • Core random-picking features work offline.
  • Rando does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Rando does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Apple may process App Store purchase, crash, or diagnostic information according to your device and App Store settings. Apple's privacy policies apply to that processing.

3. Data control and retention

You can delete configurations and clear result history inside Rando. Local data otherwise remains in the app container on your device. Deleting the app will normally delete its on-device data.

4. Children

Rando does not require an account and does not include chat, public content, behavioral advertising, or interaction between users.

5. Changes to this policy

If Rando materially changes how it handles data, we will update this policy and its “Last updated” date.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 24 日

Rando 将你的配置、偏好和近期结果保存在设备本地。我们不运营用户账户或数据服务器，Rando 也不使用广告、跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。

1. Rando 处理哪些数据

为了提供可重复使用的随机选择配置和结果历史，Rando 会在设备本地保存你主动创建的数据，例如数字范围、文字列表、图标选择、配置名称、近期结果和 App 偏好。

Rando 不会把这些数据上传到开发者服务器，也不会出售数据或将其用于广告画像。

2. 网络、分析与跟踪

  • 核心随机选择功能可离线使用。
  • Rando 不使用第三方广告或分析 SDK。
  • Rando 不进行跨 App 或网站跟踪。
  • Apple 可能根据你的设备和 App Store 设置处理购买、崩溃或诊断信息；这些处理适用 Apple 自己的隐私政策。

3. 数据控制与保留

你可以在 Rando 中删除配置并清空结果历史。其他本地数据会保留在设备的 App 容器中；删除 App 通常会同时删除设备上的 App 数据。

4. 儿童

Rando 不要求创建账户，也不包含聊天、公开内容、行为广告或用户间互动。

5. 政策变更

如果 Rando 的数据处理方式发生实质变化，我们会更新本政策及其“最后更新”日期。
