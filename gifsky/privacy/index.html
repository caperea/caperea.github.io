Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Gifsky turns videos into GIFs on your device. We do not operate user accounts or data servers, and Gifsky does not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Media you choose

Gifsky receives only the video you select through Apple’s system Photos picker. The app copies that selected video into its private app container so it can preview, trim, and convert the video locally. Gifsky does not upload your video or generated GIF to a developer server.

Generated GIFs

Generated GIFs and gallery information are stored locally in Gifsky’s app container. They leave the app only when you choose a system share, copy, or Save to Photos action. A third-party destination you select is governed by that destination’s own privacy policy.

Photos access

Video selection uses Apple’s privacy-preserving Photos picker. Gifsky requests add-only Photos permission only when you choose Save to Photos.

Accounts, analytics, advertising, and tracking

  • Gifsky does not require or provide a user account.
  • Gifsky does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Gifsky does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Gifsky does not sell personal data.

Data control and retention

You can delete individual GIFs from the gallery. Deleting Gifsky normally removes videos, GIFs, and metadata stored inside its app container. Copies you previously saved to Photos, Files, or another app are not removed by deleting Gifsky.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store, crash, or diagnostic information according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy policy. Gifsky does not receive that information through its own server.

Changes

If Gifsky materially changes how it handles data, this policy and its “Last updated” date will be updated before the new practice is represented in the App Store.
