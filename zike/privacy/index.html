隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 14 日

字课把听写记录、熟练度和复习进度保存在你的设备上。我们不运营用户账户或数据服务器，也不在 App 中使用广告、跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。

1. 字课处理哪些数据

为了提供听写、错字复习、熟练度统计和备份恢复功能，字课会在设备本地保存你主动产生的学习数据，例如逐字听写结果、错字记录、学习进度和设置。

这些数据不会由字课上传到开发者服务器，也不会被出售或用于广告画像。

2. 备份与恢复

只有当你主动导出备份时，字课才会生成备份文件，并通过 iOS 系统提供的分享或文件界面交由你选择保存位置。导入备份时，文件会在设备本地处理。请妥善保管你导出的文件；第三方存储位置受相应服务的隐私政策约束。

3. 网络、分析与跟踪

  • 核心学习功能离线可用。
  • 字课不使用第三方广告或分析 SDK。
  • 字课不进行跨 App 或网站跟踪。
  • Apple 可能依据你的设备与 App Store 设置处理购买、崩溃或诊断信息；这些处理适用 Apple 自己的隐私政策。

4. 儿童与家庭使用

字课面向家庭纸笔听写场景，不要求创建账户，也不包含聊天、公开内容、行为广告或用户间互动。家长或监护人可以和孩子一起选择练习范围并管理学习数据。

5. 数据控制与保留

学习数据保留在设备上的 App 容器中，直至你通过 App 或 iOS 管理这些数据。删除 App 通常会删除设备上的 App 数据；你另行导出的备份文件不受此操作影响。

6. 政策变更

如果字课的数据处理方式发生实质变化，我们会更新本政策及其“最后更新”日期，并在适当位置说明变化。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

ZiKe stores dictation results, proficiency, and review progress on your device. We do not operate user accounts or data servers, and the app does not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

1. Data ZiKe handles

To provide dictation, mistake review, proficiency statistics, backup, and restore, ZiKe stores learning data you create locally on your device. This may include character-level dictation results, mistake records, learning progress, and settings.

ZiKe does not upload this data to developer servers, sell it, or use it to build advertising profiles.

2. Backup and restore

ZiKe creates a backup file only when you choose to export one. The iOS sharing or file interface then lets you choose where to save it. Imported backups are processed locally on your device. You are responsible for files you export, and any third-party storage location is governed by that service's privacy policy.

3. Network access, analytics, and tracking

  • Core learning features work offline.
  • ZiKe does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • ZiKe does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Apple may process purchase, crash, or diagnostic information according to your device and App Store settings. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

4. Children and family use

ZiKe supports family-based paper dictation. It does not require an account and does not include chat, public content, behavioral advertising, or interaction between users. A parent or guardian can choose practice ranges and manage learning data with a child.

5. Data control and retention

Learning data remains in the app container on your device until you manage it through ZiKe or iOS. Deleting the app will normally delete its on-device data. Backup files you exported separately are not affected.

6. Changes to this policy

If ZiKe materially changes how it handles data, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date and provide an appropriate notice.
